💡 Tower of Hanoi Game Rules ▼

🏰 Game Guide & Rules

Move all disks from Peg 1 to Peg 3. Remember the rules: move only one disk at a time, and never place a larger disk on top of a smaller one!

🌱 Life Skills & Skilling
  • Strategic Patience: Taking time to look ahead, plan your moves, and avoid careless mistakes.
  • Recursive Logic (Divide & Conquer): Mastering how to solve 3 disks becomes the exact blueprint for solving 4, 5, or 6 disks—because big challenges are just smaller patterns repeated!
  • Mental Agility: Measuring how smoothly and efficiently your mind executes the solution once you have a clear plan.
