Classical 7-5-3 Game (Base for Pop-it Game)

🐍 This could be your python project!

🎯 Goal: Crack the pattern, make opponent unable to pop.

How to Play:

1️⃣ Players take turns removing bubbles from a single row per turn.
2️⃣ Pop bubbles individually, or click the line divider between rows to clear an entire row at once. Toggle/un-pop if you change your mind!
3️⃣ Click "Finish Turn / Confirm" after making your selection.
